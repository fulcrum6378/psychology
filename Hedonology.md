### 🟢 Pleasure

- definition: a good sense implying **fortune**
- caused by: fulfilment of a wish (dopamine)
- instances:
    - 🟢 Orgasm
    - 🟢 Happiness: Cognitive Acceptance `of anything / anyone / the world`
    - 🟢 Food
    - 🟢 Desirable Temperature Change
    - 🟢 Thrill
    - 🟢 Aesthetics

### 🔴 Pain

- definition: a bad sense implying **misery**
- caused by: failure of a wish (dopamine)
- instances:
    - 🔴 Disgust
    - 🔴 Headache
    - 🔴 Anger: Cognitive Disacceptance `of anything / anyone / the world`
    - 🔴 Physical Pain
