# Psychological Relief

The brain, like a government, has a system of strict laws it operates based on,
and if these rules are broken, the brain naturally makes the consciousness suffer!
This government is called "**Superego**" by *Sigmund Freud*.

The laws are created not by wise thinking but by aggression.
When a child perceives aggression from its caregivers, it internalises that aggression
and its superego develops rules to avoid that aggression.
Therefore, internalised aggression is the source of psychological pain.
It can also surprisingly create rewards and dreams!

> Perceived aggression → Internalised aggression / Disacceptance of the condition → Reward & Punishment

Now there are many methods devised in 20th and 21st centuries and loosely before that.
If anyone ever relieved their psychological pain,
they would have to go through a reversed version of the process I mentioned above; meaning:

> Perceived security → Internalised security / Acceptance of the condition → Indifference

Now "Perceived aggression or security" can be defined in two ways:

1. Childish way: actual aggression from caregivers
2. Adult way: beliefs, especially axiological beliefs

Adult humans often don't suffer psychologically because of actual aggression.
Actual aggression might temporarily cause anxiety and adrenaline spikes, but it won't last long.

> Humans suffer their unrealistic beliefs and unmet expectations.

As I said, the superego is a bunch of rules in the brain.
Now how would you try to change rules in a government and how powerful are you in that government?
These so-called "therapists" try to reverse the internalised-aggression process in different scales and strengths,
and using different premises.

1. *Cognitive-Behavioural Therapists* work like lawyers; they don't often get close to the king (low trust),
   but they have strong skills in persuasion and logical manipulation (cognitive restructuring).
   They work like politicians and try their best to make their so-called "patients" stop rejecting themselves.
   Because of their low power, they cannot modify rules (a person's deep beliefs) but they can make some samples
   exempted from the law.
2. *Psychoanalysts* work like ministers; they try their best to get as close to the king as possible (high trust),
   then they gain deep knowledge of the king's past, and then they have the power to modify some rules!
3. *Logotherapists* much like CBT therapists, they play with words to make a person accept the world.

These methods work more or less on average people.
But psychological work can be much greater than they try to do.
They focus on people who are under extreme distress, but what if you use it for maximum happiness?
What if we use psychology to have happier lives?
I don't believe psychological pain is a disorder but what if, in their terms,
we look for vaccines instead of medications?

If our culture gets changed in certain ways and we adopt certain philosophies,
we can theoretically become immune to psychological pain!
